About

The Story

Jaden Money is an alt-rock artist blending grungy guitars with dark, emotionally raw lyricism. Originally from the Memphis area and now based in Wilmington, NC, her music explores themes of escapism, identity, and toxic attachment. By balancing vulnerability with a sharp-edged defiance, she creates a sound that is both restless and unfiltered.

"Haunting, heavy, and unapologetically honest."

Influenced by '90s grunge and the powerful vocals of early-2000s alternative rock, Jaden's music carries the weight of the artists who came before while carving out something entirely her own. She pairs a massive vocal delivery with a heavy, grit-driven sonic landscape that mirrors the tension and release found in her lyrics.

Jaden is available for original sets, acoustic cover performances, festivals, and private bookings.
